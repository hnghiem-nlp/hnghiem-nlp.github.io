    <p>I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Professor <a href="https://users.umiacs.umd.edu/~hal3/">Hal Daumé III</a>. My research focuses on <b>Safety, Robustness and Trustworthiness in AI</b>, and on applying NLP systems to social problems. I am currently a Research Fellow at the <a href="https://www.cbai.ai/">Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative</a>, working on technical AI governance with Professor <a href="https://people.csail.mit.edu/dhm/">Dylan Hadfield-Menell</a> at MIT, and I was a <a href="https://www.matsprogram.org/">MATS</a> 9.0 &amp; 9.1 scholar with Dr. <a href="https://www.noamkolt.com/">Noam Kolt</a>, auditing how LLMs align with legal requirements. My recent and upcoming work investigates and strengthens the robustness of Large Language Models (LLMs) in tasks that require complex reasoning. Before UMD, I earned my Master's degree at the University of Southern California and conducted research in social media under <a href="https://fredzilla.github.io/">Dr. Fred Morstatter</a> and <a href="http://www.emilio.ferrara.name/">Dr. Emilio Ferrara</a>. I also worked at the <a href="https://datanetwork.org/">Children's Data Network</a> as a Data Scientist, investigating and enhancing public policy in the child welfare system.</p>
